# Privacy Policy — FinTrack

FinTrack is built with privacy as a core principle.

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** FinTrack does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- No account required
- No internet connection required
- All data stays on your device
- No analytics or tracking

## Crash Reports

If the app crashes, an optional crash report may be generated locally on your device. This report is only sent if you explicitly choose to share it. It may contain device type and OS version — no personal or financial data is ever included.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the project repository.
