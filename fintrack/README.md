# FinTrack — Wallet Balance Tracker

**FinTrack** is a free, open-source Android app for tracking your spending and managing your wallet balance. Enter your current balance, record every expense, and always know exactly how much you have left.

No account. No login. No data collection. Fully offline and private.

---

## Features

- **Wallet Balance** — Set your current wallet or account balance
- **Real-time Remaining Balance** — See how much is left as you add expenses
- **Expense History** — Full transaction log with tags, dates, and amounts
- **Analytics** — Spending charts and breakdowns by category
- **Home Screen Widgets** — See your remaining balance without opening the app
- **CSV Export** — Export your spending history anytime
- **Material You** — Dynamic theming that adapts to your system color palette
- **Multi-currency** — World currencies or custom currency symbols
- **Dark / Light / System theme**

---

## How It Works

1. Enter your wallet balance (how much money you have or want to track)
2. Record each expense as you spend
3. FinTrack shows your remaining balance in real time

That's it — no monthly periods, no daily budget calculations, no complex setup.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI**: Jetpack Compose + Material You (Material 3)
- **Architecture**: MVVM + Hilt Dependency Injection
- **Storage**: Room Database + DataStore Preferences
- **Min SDK**: 29 (Android 10+)
